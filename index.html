About Jen-Kat

Be our guest, feel your best.

A private hot spring resort made for time together.

Jen-Kat Hot Spring Resort sits in Pansol, Calamba, Laguna, the part of town known for its hot spring resorts. Both of our pools use natural hot and warm spring water, and we drain and clean them after each guest.

Inside the gates you will find a pool with a slide, a kiddie pool, six air-conditioned rooms, a kitchen, a BBQ griller, and a rooftop with a view of Mount Makiling. There is also a piano, a guitar, videoke, billiards, darts, table tennis, and a basketball court for when the water gets too relaxing.

It works for family reunions, barkada weekends, birthdays, and quiet getaways. Message us with your dates and group size, and we will help you plan the stay.

Finding us in Pansol.

From Manila Service Road in Pansol, look for Bagong Kalsada Street. The resort sits beside the railroad tracks. If you are coming from Turbina, take Bucal Bypass Road. Heading to Los Baños? Keep following Manila Service Road. The map shows the landmarks along the way.
